What it fixes about the V2 model I built:
The V2 model treats "assembly" as the primary object and "component" as a child. That forces a tree structure — an insert belongs to this boring bar. But in reality, a CNMG 432 insert fits dozens of different holders. The insert is its own thing in the crib with its own stock level, its own reorder point, its own drawer location. It's not a child of any single assembly.
Your four-table design treats every physical item as a first-class citizen in the Tools table. A boring bar body is a row. A CNMG insert is a row. A torx screw is a row. They all have their own identity, their own category, their own photos. The Components junction table then says "this boring bar uses this insert in the insert role and this screw in the screw role." What this means for ToolSnap's capture flow:
The wizard changes fundamentally.
